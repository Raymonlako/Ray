# Lido Deck Outbreak

A top-down zombie survival game set on the deck of a cruise ship at night. Open `index.html` in a browser; no build step.

- **WASD / arrows**: move · **Mouse**: aim the flashlight, click to shoot · **R** reload · **P** pause · **M** mute
- **Touch**: left thumb moves, right thumb aims and fires

## The crew

- **Mari Vega** (you), Cruise Director: security pistol and flashlight.
- **Dev Okoro**, Tiki bartender: throws burning bottles of rum. Found at the Tiki Bar.
- **Hana Sato**, Lifeguard: heals anyone nearby and shoves zombies back with her rescue can. Found at the pool.
- **Capt. Walt Brennan**: fires flares that light the dark and set the deck on fire. Found at the bow.

Walk up to a crew member to recruit them. If one goes down, stand over them to revive them.

## Demo and video

Click **Watch the crew** on the menu (or open `index.html#demo`) to watch the game play itself; press any key to take over. A recorded run is in `media/lido-deck-outbreak-gameplay.mp4`.

## Enemies

Zombies climb out of the stairwell hatches and over the rails in waves: tourists, fast crew members and tough galley chefs. The pool slows everyone down. Buffet plates heal you; shotgun shells give you a spread shot.
